## User va workflow hien tai

User chinh la Lab Coach/ngoi truc tiep san xuat lesson. Workflow duoc mo ta qua phong van:

1. Lay bai giang da ghi am hoac noi dung cua ngay.
2. Chuyen thanh transcript va loc tu dem/doan ngoai le.
3. Co dong kien thuc cot loi.
4. Viet script va chia thanh module 5-10 phut.
5. Chia noi dung thanh doan/canh, phan bo thoi luong va chon cach minh hoa.
6. Dung tool sinh hinh, tool sinh video, quiz hoac tuong tac; sau do ghep va ra soat.
